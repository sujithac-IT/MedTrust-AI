The Complete 17-Section Clinical Schema

Synthesized directly from live doctor-student-patient dialogue in real time.

01
Patient Info & Demographics

MRN, age, DOB, blood group, attending consultant, student intern.

02
Chief Presenting Complaint

Primary symptoms recorded in patient's authentic words.

03
History of Present Illness (HPI)

Chronological evolution, radiation, aggravating and relieving factors.

04
Symptoms Checklist & Severity

Categorized grading: Mild, Moderate, Severe, and Critical triage.

05
Duration & Onset Pattern

Acute vs. insidious onset, frequency, and episodic trajectory.

06
Past Medical History

Hypertension, Diabetes, Asthma, past surgical interventions.

07
Current Medications (Dynamic Table)

Interactive table: Drug, Dosage, Frequency, Route, Duration, Instructions.

08
Allergies & Adverse Drug Reactions

Food, penicillin, ACE-inhibitor cough, and severe drug alerts.

09
Family Medical History

Premature CAD, hereditary diabetes, cancer, and atopy.

10
Social & Occupational History

Lifestyle, dietary sodium compliance, sleep hygiene, shift hours.

11
Doctor Observations & Vitals

BP, Pulse, Respiratory Rate, Temp, SpO2, BMI, systemic auscultation.

12
Recommended Investigations

12-Lead ECG, 2D-Echo, TMT, HbA1c, spot UACR, endoscopy, panels.

13
Provisional Assessment (ICD-10)

Primary diagnostic impression coded with standard ICD-10 designations.

14
Comprehensive Treatment Plan

Drug initiations, titrations, emergency sublingual SOS guidelines.

15
Scheduled Review & Red Flags

Emergency warning triggers (seek immediate ER) and clinic reviews.

16
Missing / Incomplete Info

AI-flagged omitted clinical parameters requiring lab follow-up.

17
Uncertain / Needs Clarification

Ambiguities flagged for senior consultant counter-review.
