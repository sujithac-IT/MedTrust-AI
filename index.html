Core Features

25 AI-Powered Modules

Every module of MediTrust AI is designed to bring intelligence, trust, and continuity to healthcare.

New

Smart Patient Registration

Register with Aadhaar, Face Recognition, or QR Code. AI auto-creates your secure profile in under 2 minutes.

AI Symptom Intelligence Agent

Before meeting the doctor, describe symptoms via text or voice. AI conducts a clinical interview and generates a structured summary.

AI Smart Appointment Booking

Search by doctor, specialty, or symptoms. View real-time slots. Book hospital visits or video consultations instantly.

AI Queue Intelligence

Live queue position, estimated consultation time, and waiting duration — all before you leave home.

AI Video Consultation

Secure video consults with live speech-to-text, prescription generation, and automatic follow-up scheduling.

Core

AI Medical Scribe

Doctor speaks naturally. AI extracts medicine, dosage, frequency, and generates a digital prescription instantly.

AI Healthcare Copilot

Doctors control the system by voice. "Show previous MRI." "Compare blood reports." "Generate discharge summary."

Star

Patient Digital Twin

A continuously evolving AI model of each patient — capturing history, labs, lifestyle, wearables, and predicting risks.

Hospital Digital Twin

Real-time monitoring of beds, ICU, doctors, ambulances, pharmacy, and patient flow in a unified command view.

Clinical Decision Atlas

Compare multiple evidence-based treatment pathways with recovery probability, cost, risk, and patient preference analysis.

Unique

AI Trust Engine

Measures and improves doctor-patient trust using communication quality, adherence, and satisfaction metrics.

AI Medical Memory

"Summarize this patient's history." AI instantly provides diagnoses, surgeries, allergies, and medication history.

AI Medical Translator

Real-time multilingual translation. Doctor speaks English, patient hears Tamil. Patient replies Tamil, doctor gets English.

Offline + Online Intelligence

Designed for rural hospitals. Local AI, local speech recognition, automatic sync when internet is restored.

WhatsApp Health Companion

Prescription, reminders, diet, exercises, follow-ups — all delivered via WhatsApp. Patients ask questions, AI answers.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

AI Continuum Care

Personalized recovery plans, smart medication reminders, adherence tracking, recovery scoring, adaptive care plans — until complete recovery.

Auto Follow-up Management

"Visit after two weeks." AI books the appointment, sends reminders, and adds it to your health calendar automatically.

AI Emergency Assistant

One-tap emergency mode. AI assesses symptoms, alerts staff, notifies family, shares location, and guides first aid.

AI Predictive Healthcare

Predicts heart disease, stroke, diabetes complications, readmission risk, and ICU requirements before they occur.

AI Nurse Agent

Reminds nurses of medication schedules, vital sign monitoring, bedside rounds, critical alerts, and patient transfers.

AI Hospital Command Center

Live insights into patient flow, emergencies, ICU occupancy, doctor workload, pharmacy, and revenue analytics.

AI Knowledge Graph

Connects Patient → Disease → Medication → Lab Results → Doctor → Follow-up → Recovery for intelligent search.

AI Fraud Detection

Detects duplicate billing, insurance fraud, prescription anomalies, and identity fraud with AI pattern analysis.

Women's Health Intelligence

Life-stage care from menstrual health and pregnancy companion to menopause wellness and women's safety SOS.

AI Reputation Index

Measures waiting time, patient trust, clinical outcomes, communication quality, and readmission rates continuously.
