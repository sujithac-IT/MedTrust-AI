##  Key Innovations & Core Features

###  1. 3D Holographic Product Launch Landing Page
* **Fluid 3D Holographic Globe Canvas**: Interactive particle sphere with dynamic connecting node mesh and ambient glowing energy rings.
* **Apple-Style Hero Presentation**: Animated ECG heartbeat waveform, connected hospital telemetry counters, interactive statistics, and product demo video modal.

###  2. Universal AI Emergency Response Protocol (108 SOS)
* Accessible globally from every page via the red pulsing **`🚨 108 SOS`** button.
* **8 Clinical Triage Categories**: Cardiac & Chest Pain, Respiratory Distress, Stroke & Paralysis, Trauma & Hemorrhage, Anaphylaxis, Convulsions, Maternal Emergency, and Toxicity/Poisoning.
* **GIS Live Telemetry Radar**: Nearest hospital locator, automatic doctor/family alert, and simulated live ambulance tracking map.

###  3. Multi-Role Authentication & Session Engine
* **6 Dedicated Role Dashboards**:
  *  **Patient Hub**: Digital twin score, daily medicine schedule, side effects logger, and medical history.
  *  **Doctor Portal**: Live voice AI Medical Scribe, patient risk queue, treatment comparison tool, and digital prescription generator.
  *  **Nurse Station**: Ward bed telemetry (Beds 101–112), vitals recorder, doctor orders execution checklist.
  *  **Pharmacist Desk**: Digital prescription dispense queue, drug stock inventory, AI drug interaction checker.
  *  **Command Center**: Hospital operational telemetry, ICU bed occupancy, revenue analytics, patient flow.
  *  **Family Portal**: Patient recovery trajectory, medicine adherence timeline, live emergency location tracking.
* **4 Authentication Methods**: Email & Password, Google OAuth, Mobile OTP (simulated), Face Recognition camera scan.

###  4. Digital Prescription & PDF Report Generator
* Official hospital-branded digital prescription cards complete with ICD-10 diagnosis codes, Rx drug table, doctor digital signature, QR code verification hash, and print/PDF export optimization (`window.print()`).

###  5. HD Telehealth Video Consultation Suite
* WebRTC video consultation dialog featuring webcam stream preview, call controls (Mute, Camera, Screen Sharing), live AI Scribe transcript sidebar, and one-click prescription generation.

###  6. AI Integrated Health Calendar
* Auto-populated schedule combining doctor appointments, medication timings, lab tests, vaccinations, and diet/exercise plans.

###  7. WhatsApp Companion Channel
* Standalone WhatsApp Web interface providing 24×7 automated bot check-ins, medicine reminders, and instant digital prescription delivery.

##  Technology Stack

* **Frontend**: React 18, React Router v6, HTML5 Canvas 3D Graphics
* **Styling**: Google Material Design 3, Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties, FontAwesome Pro
* **AI & NLP Engine**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API + Intelligent Clinical NLP Triage Engine
* **Backend & Storage**: Firebase Authentication, Firestore Real-time Database, Firebase Storage
* **Build Tooling**: Vite 5, Node.js
